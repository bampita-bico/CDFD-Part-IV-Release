Flux Limitation`. On 2026-08-18, direct scope overlaps were consolidated and
13 predecessor source/PDF pairs were moved to the separate local archive
created on 2026-08-24. The historical Part IV DOI identifies the
pre-consolidation 58-paper archive; a new versioned deposit is required before
it can identify this active 45-paper release.

`PART_IV_METHODS_AND_CLAIM_BOUNDARY.md` companion. The notation is a
cross-domain modelling language, not a common material mechanism or an

result.
