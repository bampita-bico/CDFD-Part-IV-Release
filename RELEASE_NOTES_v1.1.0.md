### Claim discipline

- Removed `MUJJABI_UNIVERSAL_LAWS_AND_TESTS.md` from the active surface

- Every active manuscript expected to carry observable maps, scale, native baseline, and explicit falsifier

### Empirical honesty

First completed field-data check (USGS 01646500, next-day discharge, 2022–2024 chronological holdout):

- CDFD/AFL-style extension reduced RMSE by **0.045%** (below the predeclared 1% threshold)
- Bootstrap lower bound for MSE reduction was **negative**
- Result: **no demonstrated predictive improvement** on this declared task
- Scope: one gauge; does not validate or falsify CDFD/AFL outside that task
